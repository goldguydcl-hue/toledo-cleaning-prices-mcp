## Commercial & office cleaning (janitorial): ballpark market estimate
"Columbus, OH" is outside Simply Clean Toledo's listed service area (Toledo, OH and nearby Lucas, Wood and Fulton County, OH and Monroe County, MI communities). The general published ranges below still apply as a rough guide.
**Ballpark: $400–$2,000 per visit**

- Angi per-visit range for 5,000–20,000 sq ft: **$400–$2,000 per visit** (national).
- Angi's medical rate of $0.20–$0.50/sq ft × 10,000 sq ft works out to $2,000–$5,000 per visit. Angi publishes both figures and they differ; the size range above is the main guide.
- HomeGuide monthly contract figure for 8,000–10,000 sq ft (based on 3 visits a week): $650–$2,000+ per month. Sources differ a lot here; recurring contracts are usually priced lower per visit than one-time cleans.
- Hourly (Angi, medical): $35–$80 per cleaner-hour.
- Toledo-area reference (Thumbtack's Toledo page): office cleaning averages about $200 per clean; about $120 for 1,000 sq ft or less; about $880 for 9,000–10,000 sq ft; commercial cleaners charge about $25–$90 an hour.
- Local labor context (BLS, Toledo metro, May 2025): janitors earn a mean $17.64/hr (median $16.94). That is worker pay, not the price a company bills.
