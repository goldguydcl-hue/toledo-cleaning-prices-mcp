## Boat cleaning: ballpark market estimate
Oregon, OH is in Simply Clean Toledo's service area (Lucas County).
**Ballpark: $430–$720 per job**

- Boat full detail (Deckhand, 65 US businesses): $18–$30/ft, typical $22.50/ft.
