## Commercial & office cleaning (janitorial): ballpark market estimate
Toledo, OH is in Simply Clean Toledo's service area (Lucas County).
**Ballpark: $150–$600 per visit**

- Angi per-visit range for 1,000–5,000 sq ft: **$150–$600 per visit** (national).
- Angi's office rate of $0.10–$0.20/sq ft × 3,000 sq ft works out to $300–$600 per visit. Angi publishes both figures and they differ; the size range above is the main guide.
- HomeGuide monthly contract figure for 2,000–4,000 sq ft (based on 3 visits a week): $250–$800 per month. Sources differ a lot here; recurring contracts are usually priced lower per visit than one-time cleans.
- Angi per-visit range for weekly service: $150–$600.
- Hourly (Angi, office): $20–$80 per cleaner-hour.
- Toledo-area reference (Thumbtack's Toledo page): office cleaning averages about $200 per clean; about $120 for 1,000 sq ft or less; about $880 for 9,000–10,000 sq ft; commercial cleaners charge about $25–$90 an hour.
- Local labor context (BLS, Toledo metro, May 2025): janitors earn a mean $17.64/hr (median $16.94). That is worker pay, not the price a company bills.
